Schedule change

All-hands moves to Thursdays, starting March

Starting March 5, the company all-hands runs Thursdays at 16:00 UTC instead of Mondays. The format and length don't change.

Why

Mondays collided with regional holidays too often, pushing recordings to Tuesday. Thursday keeps it live for more people.

  • Calendar invites update automatically — no action needed.
  • Can't attend live? The recording and notes post within the hour.
