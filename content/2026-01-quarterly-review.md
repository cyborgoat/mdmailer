# Q4 in review

Closing out the quarter with strong growth across the board and a few lessons learned along the way.

## By the numbers

| Metric | Q3 | Q4 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active users | 42,000 | 58,500 | +39% |
| Monthly revenue | $210k | $284k | +35% |
| Support tickets | 1,180 | 960 | -19% |
| Median response time | 6h | 3.5h | -42% |

## Wins

1. Shipped the new onboarding flow, cutting time-to-first-value from *9 minutes* to *2 minutes*
2. Closed the top three churn drivers identified in the Q3 survey
3. Support response time improved after moving to the new ticketing tool

## What didn't go well

- The mobile app release slipped by six weeks due to underestimated App Store review turnaround
- We under-resourced the docs rewrite; it's now targeted for Q1

## Goals for next quarter

- [x] Finalize Q1 roadmap with engineering and design
- [ ] Ship the mobile app to both app stores
- [ ] Complete the documentation rewrite
- [ ] Run the next customer satisfaction survey

> As always, the full board deck and raw data are in the shared drive — this email is just the summary.

Thanks for another strong quarter. On to Q1.
