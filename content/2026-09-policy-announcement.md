Starting **October 1, 2026**, expense reports are due within **30 days** of the
purchase date, and receipts are required for anything over **$25** (down from $75).

## What's changing

| Area | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt threshold | $75 | $25 |
| Submission window | 60 days | 30 days |
| Approval | Manager only | Manager + Finance for > $2,000 |

## What you need to do

- Submit any outstanding reports from before October 1 under the **current** rules by September 30.
- After that, the new thresholds apply automatically in the expense tool — no setup needed.
